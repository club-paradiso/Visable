# VISABLE Translator & Reviewer Guide: Immigration Legal Information Guidelines

This guide establishes linguistic, semantic, and administrative quality standards for translators, editors, and legal information reviewers working on VISABLE.

---

## 1. Mission & Voice

VISABLE guides foreign residents through complex administrative procedures in South Korea. The voice should be:

- **Empathetic & Clear**: Foreign applicants are often anxious about administrative deadlines and visa status. Language should be welcoming and direct.
- **Legally Accurate**: Never sacrifice legal precision for brevity. A missing distinction between "original" and "copy" can lead to visa rejection.
- **Plain Language First**: Explain bureaucratic jargon in simple terms before presenting official statutory citations.

---

## 2. Core Translation Principles

### A. Statutory Supremacy & The Dual-Language Rule
Official Korean immigration administration is conducted exclusively in Korean. Therefore:
- **Rule**: Official Korean form titles and statutory names (e.g. `별지 제34호 통합신청서`) must ALWAYS remain in the text alongside the translation.
- **Pattern**: `[Translated Plain-Language Name] ([Official Korean Statutory Title])`
- **Example**: `Unified Application Form (통합신청서 별지 제34호)`

### B. Preserving Conditionality & Modals
Administrative law distinguishes sharply between mandatory requirements, conditional prerequisites, and optional supporting documents. Translators must NEVER soften or escalate requirement strength:

| Requirement Level | Korean Term | English Standard | Chinese Standard | Vietnamese Standard | Indonesian Standard |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mandatory** | 필수 제출 / 반드시 지참 | Mandatory / Required | 必须提交 / 必备材料 | Bắt buộc / Phải nộp | Wajib diserahkan |
| **Conditional** | 해당자 한함 / 조건부 제출 | Conditional / If applicable | 视情况提交 / 符合条件者 | Áp dụng cho đối tượng cụ thể | Jika berlaku |
| **Optional** | 참고 서류 / 권장 | Optional / Recommended | 参考材料 / 建议提供 | Tài liệu tham khảo | Dokumen pendukung |
| **In-Person Only** | 본인 출석 필수 (대리 불가) | In-person attendance required (No proxy) | 本人必须到场 (不可代办) | Bắt buộc trực tiếp có mặt (Không ủy quyền) | Wajib hadir langsung (Tidak bisa diwakilkan) |

## 4. Reviewer Checklist (Do's and Don'ts)

### DO:
- [O] Verify that statutory Korean titles appear in parentheses or adjacent spans.
- [O] Ensure that procedural distinctions between "Visa Issuance" (overseas consulate) and "Change of Status" (in-country) are strictly maintained.
- [O] Use standard diacritics in Vietnamese (`Tiếng Việt`) and natural phrasing in Indonesian (`Bahasa Indonesia`).
- [O] Check that numbers, dates, and currency symbols (`₩` / `KRW`) match Korean official fees.

### DON'T:
- [X] Do NOT use colloquial or informal slang for administrative procedures.
- [X] Do NOT translate alphanumeric status codes (e.g. translate the explanation of `D-2`, but never translate `D-2` itself).
- [X] Do NOT delete statutory citations or footnote markers (e.g. `별지 제34호`, `제17조`).
- [X] Do NOT guarantee visa approval in explanatory copy (all grants are subject to immigration officer discretion).
