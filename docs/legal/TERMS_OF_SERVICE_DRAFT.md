## 1. Purpose

This draft describes the intended terms under which a user may access
and use the Paradiso website (immigration / stay administration
information access platform) and the Paradiso.ai feature (AI-assisted
administrative information tool). It exists so that an operator and
reviewing counsel can see, in one place, what the product is and is
**not**, in plain language.

## 2. Scope

These draft terms cover:

- Access to and use of the Paradiso website (`index.html` and
  associated static assets).
- Access to and use of the Paradiso.ai assistant (`ai.html` and the
  associated backend `/api/ask` endpoint).
- Use of the in-browser reminder feature.

They do **not** cover:

- Third-party websites that the product may link to, including official
  government portals. Those sites have their own terms.
- Any future paid feature, account-based feature, or notification
  channel (email, SMS, push). Such features would require an updated
  set of terms before launch.

## 4. Informational purpose; no legal representation

Paradiso and Paradiso.ai are **information tools**. They exist to help
the user navigate publicly available administrative information about
immigration and stay procedures.

Use of the product does **not** create a lawyer–client relationship, an
administrative-scrivener engagement, or any other form of professional
representation between the user and the operator. The product does not
file applications on the user's behalf, does not make administrative
decisions, and does not bind any government agency.

## 5. No guarantee of accuracy, completeness, or currentness

Administrative rules, manuals, and government practices change.
Paradiso draws on a mixture of:

- official manuals
- publicly available data
- internally normalized data
- law-grounding metadata (currently disabled by default and not
  considered production-ready)

Even with reasonable care, information available through Paradiso may
be incomplete, out of date, ambiguous, or wrong for the user's specific
situation. The operator does **not** warrant that the information
returned is accurate, complete, current, or fit for any particular
purpose, except to the extent that such a disclaimer is prohibited by
applicable law.

In particular:

- Paradiso.ai answers may be wrong, incomplete, or out of date.
- Citation links and references shown in answers are not guaranteed to
  be live, accurate, or applicable.
- Citation verification is currently partial and not fully wired.
- High-risk decisions (status renewal, change of status, deportation
  risk, employment eligibility, family-status filings, residency time
  counting, and similar) must be verified with the relevant official
  agency or a qualified professional before action is taken.

## 6. Official source verification

The user agrees to treat Paradiso as a starting point, not as the final
authority. Before relying on any specific procedural step shown by the
product, the user is responsible for verifying the rule against an
official source, such as:

- the relevant immigration / administrative authority's official
  website
- the official manual referenced in the answer, where one is cited
- a qualified lawyer, administrative scrivener, or licensed
  professional in the relevant jurisdiction

## 7. User responsibility

The user is responsible for:

- the truthfulness and accuracy of any information they enter, into
  Paradiso.ai questions or into reminder notes;
- choosing not to share information more sensitive than the question
  actually requires (the product encourages data minimization);
- following the law of their jurisdiction;
- using the product only for lawful, personal informational purposes,
  unless the operator separately grants other rights in writing.

## 8. Prohibited misuse

The user agrees not to:

- attempt to use Paradiso or Paradiso.ai to commit fraud, immigration
  fraud, document forgery, or any other unlawful act;
- enter information about third parties that the user is not entitled
  to share;
- attempt to extract personal data of other users or of the operator's
  staff;
- scrape, mass-query, or otherwise abuse the service in a way that
  degrades it for other users;
- attempt to reverse engineer, jailbreak, or otherwise circumvent the
  safety constraints of the AI assistant;
- redistribute the AI assistant's answers as authoritative legal advice
  or as official government interpretation;
- use the service to harass, defame, or target any individual.
