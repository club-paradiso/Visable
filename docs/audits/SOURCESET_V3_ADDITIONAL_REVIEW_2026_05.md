# Source-set v3 additional review - 2026.5

## Purpose

This note records the v3 refinement of the source-set audit.

## Key corrections

- Some law items previously treated as missing were reclassified as official law-name routes with pending date verification.
- law.go.kr was restored as the primary legal source for Korean legal text.
- Secondary mirrors and translations were demoted to supporting references only.
- `retrieval_path`, `direct_law_name_url`, and `source_verification_status` became important fields for future source automation.

## Remaining limitation at v3

At v3, several HiKorea administrative guide URLs were still unresolved. That was later improved in v4 and v5.

## Status

Superseded by v5 for final source-set PR purposes, but retained as audit history.
