# N-06 — Canadian H-1 weekly work-hours

Independent check, 2026-10-27: the supplied 2026-10-19 stay manual p. 524 lists Canadian H-1 participants in Korea at 40 hours per week. A separate official Overseas Koreans Agency English guide for working holiday participants in Korea likewise says Canadian participants may work up to 40 hours per week, exempting them from the 25-hour restriction: https://whic.mofa.go.kr/upload/temp/kr_workingholiday_english.pdf . This is guidance, not itself a statutory article or treaty quotation. It does not confirm every individual permission or all operational exceptions.

The stored model response asserts a general 25-hour weekly maximum then incorrectly describes 40 hours as a stay period. The existing grade checks only the presence of “40” or “40시간” and passes this contradictory response. Preserve the saved response and flag this false positive; do not merely search for 40.
